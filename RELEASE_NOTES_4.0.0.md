# Beauty Mirror 4.0.0 — Pond workshop edition

- Replaced the previous synthetic water treatment with a restrained procedural pond based on the
  supplied workshop references.
- Added a configurable 3–30 second transformation, defaulting to 10 seconds.
- Beauty corrections now accumulate with looking time; pond water is visible from the beginning.
- Closing either settings panel or tapping outside commits the look, hides chrome and restarts the
  transformation.
- Added automatic visitor-session restart after sustained absence and direct tracker hand-off.
- Added a subtle no-face invitation and transformation progress presentation for exhibition use.
- Added emergency pond quality gating and reduced optional work during the early reveal.
- Added `make workshop` and detailed workshop documentation.
- Bumped app version to 4.0.0 (versionCode 19).
