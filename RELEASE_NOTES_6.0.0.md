# Beauty Mirror 6.0.0 — Fluid Sky Pond

- Replaced the grey pond/cut-out composition with a sky-blue procedural water surface.
- Added continuous layered ripples, sky reflections and a sun highlight near the top of the idle pond.
- The full beautified camera image now merges into the pond; the face is never hard-clipped.
- Pond wave motion, speculars and water veil remain after the reveal finishes (the pond no longer disappears).
- Added a transient fluid vortex and expanding arrival wave centered on each newly detected face.
- Added curator controls for camera blend, deformation and vortex strength.
- Added Sky pond, Silk water, Fluid and Gallery scene presets.
- Removed early pond-only face-mask rendering; masks are generated only for active beauty passes.
- Preserved configurable 3–30 second reveal and automatic restart for every new visitor.
- Updated English/French copy, pond diagrams, defaults and migration schema.
